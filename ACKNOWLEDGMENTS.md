# Acknowledgments

BetterBacoor preserves the transferred repository history and the CC0 public-domain dedication of the BetterLocalGov starter.

The foundation selectively adopts ideas and tooling from:

- [BetterLocalGov](https://github.com/iyanski/betterlocalgov), the reusable React/Vite starter lineage;
- [Kapwa](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@bettergov/kapwa), the civic interface package retained for compatible styling; and
- the [BetterLGU directory](https://github.com/jmacj/better-lgu-directory), which records BetterBacoor’s canonical repository and maintainer.

All Bacoor civic information must be independently verified against authoritative Bacoor sources. Upstream sample content is not evidence for Bacoor records.
