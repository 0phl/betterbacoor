Resolve former barangay names against the merger record. Hold the ambiguous Bayanan location before mapping it. The separately listed [Bayanan PMDT satellite treatment center](https://ntp.doh.gov.ph/view-facility/?id=925) has a San Nicolas II address and a specialist TB role; a similar name does not prove that these are the same facility.

The city's [June 27, 2025 opening report](https://bacoor.gov.ph/latest-news/icymi-blessing-of-new-super-health-center-in-bacoor-city/) identifies a new Super Health Center in Maliksi 2, opened June 26. It announces laboratory, ECG, pharmacy, outpatient and minor-surgery services. This establishes an opening and announced services, not today's availability or fees.

The [City Health Office page](https://bacoor.gov.ph/office-of-the-city-health-officer/) lists (046) 435-3420 and Monday-Friday, 8 AM-5 PM. The phone is corroborated by the 2026 Charter's final directory page. Do not copy the central office's hours onto individual RHUs. Match existing health-directory records only where facility identity is clear.

## 2. Barangay document fee exemptions

[Ordinance 366-2024](https://bacoorcitysp.com/wp-content/PDF/ORD/2024/366-2024.pdf?_t=1712817932), PDF pages 2-4, covers barangay clearance and barangay ID fee exemptions for qualifying Bacoor residents: indigents, students, senior citizens, solo parents, first-time jobseekers and PWDs. Relevant scanned pages were visually inspected.

Preserve the definitions: seniors are 60 or older; the student category excludes the listed postgraduate courses. The PWD provision does not require possession of an ID in every case. Sections 3.1 and 4 express indigency criteria differently; obtain clarification before turning them into an automated eligibility decision. Do not invent a single documentary checklist, issuance time, or an exemption for unrelated certificates.

The city also published a [May 22, 2025 clearance-fee reminder](https://bacoor.gov.ph/announcement/good-news-city-ordinance-366-2024-o-libreng-barangay-clearance-certificate-sa-senior-citizen-indigent-solo-parents-students-pwd-at-first-time-job-seekers/). No amending ordinance was verified in this pass; this was not an exhaustive legal-currency review. The practical feature should explain the ordinance, offer questions to ask the barangay, and link to its exact pages.

## 3. Former-name lookup

[Ordinance 275-2023](https://bacoorcitysp.com/wp-content/PDF/ORD/2023/275%202023.pdf?_t=1695093654), page 7, explicitly maps 18 merged groups and five renamings. The table was visually inspected. Examples: Sineguelasan/Banalo/Alima became Sinbanali; Kaingin/Digman became Kaingin Digman; former Ligas 3 became Ligas 2; former Zapote 5 became Zapote 3.

The [PSA third-quarter 2023 update](https://psa.gov.ph/content/third-quarter-2023-psgc-updates-conversion-new-city-merging-44-barangays-renaming-five) records ratification on July 29, 2023. A [city anniversary report](https://bacoor.gov.ph/latest-news/icymi-bacoor-city-celebrates-the-2nd-year-anniversary-of-barangay-merging/) corroborates the change from 73 to 47 barangays.

Use a deliberate former-name search with a visible explanation. For example, former Ligas 2 belongs to current Ligas 1, whereas current Ligas 2 was formerly Ligas 3. Never silently resolve an ambiguous name. Section 7 leaves hall-location determination to the new barangay officials, so old hall locations are not automatically current. This lookup would explain names, not determine jurisdiction from an address.

## 4. Assistance and accessible office details

The [2026 Citizen's Charter](https://bacoor.gov.ph/downloads/arta/CitizensCharter2026.pdf) is preserved locally at `public/documents/bacoor-citizens-charter-2026.pdf`.

- PDF pages 1056-1063, printed section 36.3 onward: assistance for people in crisis, including medical/burial assistance, case studies, referrals and indigency certification. The office is described as Bacoor Action Centers 1-6 and the main center's Special Assistance Unit.
- Pages 1064-1069: disaster-relief procedures, not a live shelter list.
- Page 1202, visually reviewed: City Health, Sagip Buhay Recovery and City Health Center Building, (046) 435-3420; OSCA, People's Center Building, 09952467450; PDAO, People's Center Building, 09974308392; veterinary office, Animal Shelter/Pound, Ibaba, Salinas 1, 0966-827-0252.

These can support sourced citywide office cards. Recheck complete service sections before authoring requirements. The older [2023 Charter](https://bacoor.gov.ph/downloads/arta/CitizensCharter2023.pdf) contains a different SSAC directory; its assignments must not populate the current page. Current BAC 1-6 barangay coverage was not verified. Program-specific staff contacts should not become general assistance hotlines.

## 5. Garbage schedules and emergency information

The city published [District 1](https://bacoor.gov.ph/announcement/garbage-collection-master-schedule-district-1/) and [District 2](https://bacoor.gov.ph/announcement/garbage-collection-master-schedule-district-2/) master-schedule announcements on December 31, 2025, effective January 5, 2026. The accessible text confirms the start date but does not expose the schedule rows. The image galleries still need transcription and visual checking, followed by a search for amendments.

Later operational reports, such as the [May 20, 2026 collection post](https://bacoor.gov.ph/latest-news/bacoor-city-announces-wednesday-garbage-collection-schedule-no-backlog/), describe particular dates. Do not convert them into permanent weekly reminders. Collection may vary by subdivision or street; a barangay-only schedule could mislead residents.

The [August 9, 2026 evacuation monitoring post](https://bacoor.gov.ph/latest-news/evacuation-centers-monitoring-update-2/) is a dated event report. It does not establish open shelters today. Keep emergency contacts and preparedness guides; add destinations only when their current purpose and location are confirmed. Treat any future advisory as dated content with an expiry/review policy, not a live feed.

## Implementation and maintenance

Store source URL, source date when available, exact PDF page, geographic scope, original barangay label and verification notes per record. Separate a facility's location from the residents it serves. Missing hours, coordinates, requirements and contacts must stay missing until sourced.

Use understated sections: Barangay details, Health services, Barangay documents, and Help and assistance. Present the useful information in BetterBacoor with compact source links. Prefer these practical additions over more generic external links or historical statistics that use obsolete barangay boundaries.

Suggested order: former-name lookup and clearance guide; carefully matched health cards; current social-assistance guides; verified route-level garbage schedules. Research gaps are explicit work items, not production facts.
